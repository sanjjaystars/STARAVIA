What we build

Three things, one small team

ThinkStick is the product we own. Web and app development is client work we take on alongside it.

Hardware

ThinkStick

Offline AI hardware — a Pi-based device that runs models on-device, with no cloud dependency and no internet required to work.

  • On-device inferenceQuantized models tuned to run locally, with no round trip to a server.
  • USB gadget modePlugs in and presents itself directly — no separate app or driver install needed.
  • Offline by designBuilt for places and use cases where connectivity isn't guaranteed.
  • Firmware we controlCustom embedded builds, so the hardware and the model stay in sync.
Web

Web development

Product sites, dashboards, and client-facing web apps — built to load fast and hold up under real traffic.

  • Marketing & product sitesFast, SEO-ready front ends for launches and landing pages.
  • Internal dashboardsAdmin panels and tools your team actually uses daily.
  • Full-stack buildsFront end, API, and database, handled as one connected system.
  • Ongoing supportWe stay on after launch for fixes, features, and upkeep.
App

App development

Mobile and cross-platform apps — from a first MVP to a product ready for the app stores.

  • Cross-platform buildsOne codebase targeting iOS and Android where it makes sense.
  • MVP deliveryScoped builds to get an early version in front of real users fast.
  • API & backend integrationApps wired into your existing services, or ones we build alongside.
  • Store-ready releaseWe handle the packaging, signing, and submission process.
About Staravia

A small team, shipping our own hardware and other people's software.

Staravia runs two kinds of work side by side. ThinkStick is our own product — offline AI hardware we design, build, and support ourselves. Web and app development is where we take on outside projects, building sites, dashboards, and apps for teams who need a working product, not just a proposal.

Interns and new hires work directly on whichever of these is live at the time — there's no separate "practice" track. What you build during an internship with us is what ends up shipped.
